What it does

Every detail, exactly how you want it.

⚡
Zero latency
Quotes are fetched and cached in the background on your previous visit so that the moment ChatGPT loads, your content is already there.
🕰
Literary time
Choose digital or literary time display. Three Forty Seven PM instead of 3:47 PM, a small detail that makes the page feel considered.
✦
Smart filtering
Quotes over 120 characters are automatically skipped and a new one is fetched. What appears is always concise enough to read at a glance.
◯
Full control
Set a custom message, leave it empty to remove the field entirely, or switch it off completely. No placeholders, no residue.
Modes
